## 🔍 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Key design and content questions explored during development:
- **What information do recruiters look for first?** → Led to a strong hero section with a clear role title and skill tags.
- **How do I best present projects?** → Created a dedicated Projects section with visual cards and descriptions.
- **What makes a portfolio stand out?** → Focused on clean UI, concise messaging, and visible proof of skills.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Insight 1:** A concise hero section with role title + skill tags (SQL, Python, Power BI, Excel) immediately communicates value to recruiters.
- **Insight 2:** Separating Projects and Skills into distinct sections improves readability and lets visitors quickly assess fit.
- **Insight 3:** Linking directly to LinkedIn and GitHub from the landing page increases professional credibility.
- **Insight 4:** A mobile-responsive design ensures accessibility across all devices — critical for job applications reviewed on phones.
